Book 1
Fruit of the Spirit · Love

The Lightkeeper's Apprentice

"The shadows aren't defeated by fighting them. They disappear when someone learns how to shine."

Twelve-year-old Avery has always wanted to be a Lightkeeper — someone who carries light into the dark corners of Elarion. When she finally gets her chance to journey with her grandfather Pops, she discovers that carrying light isn't about being brave enough, strong enough, or good enough. It's about learning what the light is actually for.

Set in the golden city of Solhaven and the dimming town of Brighthollow, The Lightkeeper's Apprentice is a story about belonging, about the kind of love that sees people clearly, and about a small dragon named Luma who is always closer than Avery realizes.

Book 2
Fruit of the Spirit · Joy

An Oasis in the Darklands

"Joy doesn't mean the dark isn't real. It means the light is more real."

When Pops receives word of a village deep in the Darklands where the Ever Song has gone nearly silent, Avery and her companions set out on their most dangerous journey yet. Grayfen is a place where laughter has faded, where lanterns have gone dark, and where people have forgotten there was ever anything to hope for.

But the Creator doesn't send Lightkeepers into the dark to bring Him somewhere He's never been. He sends them to help people notice where He already is. And what they discover in Grayfen might change the way Avery understands the light she carries — and the dragon on her shoulder who never left.

Book 3
Fruit of the Spirit · Peace

The Stillness That Holds

"Peace isn't the absence of pressure. It's the presence of the One you remain with."

At the edge of Elarion, where the Glasswater Run forms the border with the Darklands, the trading town of Stillhaven is fracturing. Something is stirring in the tension between neighbors, distorting what people hear, turning misunderstanding into fear. And Avery — who has learned love and learned joy — is about to discover that being calm when everything is pressing in is the hardest thing the Ever Song has ever asked of her.

In the third book of the Ever Song Chronicles, Avery and her companions face a threat that doesn't attack with force — it attacks with noise. And the only answer is a stillness that doesn't come from trying harder, but from something much older and steadier than fear.

Book 4
Fruit of the Spirit · Patience

Slow Light in Deep Places

"Patience isn't the absence of wanting. It's the presence of trust when nothing is moving."

Beneath the Amber Peaks, carved into the mountain's deep stone, the city of Deepward has been waiting for a very long time. The people there haven't lost the Ever Song — they just can't quite remember what they were waiting for. And into that silence, something subtle has crept: the Clockmaker, a servant of Umbros who doesn't push or threaten. It simply makes the waiting feel longer than it is.

When Avery and her companions descend into the underground city, they find a community fractured into factions, each convinced they're the ones still holding the light. The hardest thing Avery has ever faced isn't darkness that presses in — it's silence that doesn't move. And learning to stay when nothing seems to be happening might be the most important thing the Ever Song has ever asked her to do.
